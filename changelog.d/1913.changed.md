- **`memory_search` ranks by reciprocal rank fusion (#1913).** Natural-language
  queries now match notes that lack some of the query's words: FTS runs an AND
  of every word first and falls back to an OR of the content words, bounded at
  200 rows. Lexical, semantic, entity and recency results are each ranked and
  fused with `sum(w / (60 + rank))` (`UA_MEMORY_RRF_K`). Salience, feedback and
  explicit upvotes adjust the score by at most 20 percent, limiting their
  influence without guaranteeing that relevance order is preserved. Access
  counts and automatic recall counts are still recorded but no longer rank
  (`UA_MEMORY_WEIGHT_ACCESS` is deprecated).
  One- and two-word queries get semantic recall, with the similarity floor
  raised from 0.55 to 0.65 (`UA_MEMORY_SEMANTIC_MIN_SIMILARITY`). Each
  `explanation` adds per-leg `ranks`, `rrf` and `prior`. Session-packet memory
  matches any title word, ranked by bm25. On the frozen synthetic 168-note
  evaluation fixture (`scripts/eval_memory_1913.py`, 2026-10-06), hybrid
  recall@10 rose from 0.58 to 1.00 and MRR from 0.42 to 0.93.
- **Deprecated: `UA_MEMORY_WEIGHT_ACCESS` (#1913).** It is ignored; the
  `UA_MEMORY_WEIGHT_FTS`, `_SEMANTIC`, `_ENTITY` and `_RECENCY` weights now
  scale a rank contribution and have new defaults (1.0, 1.0, 1.0, 0.2).
