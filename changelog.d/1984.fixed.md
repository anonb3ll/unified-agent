- **Stopping an ACP session stops its tool subprocesses (#1984).** ACP agents now start in their own process group. Ending, cancelling or failing a session sends SIGTERM to the whole group and, after the grace period, SIGKILL to anything still running. Before this, tool processes the agent had spawned outlived the session and could keep writing to the worktree, including during the end-of-turn checkpoint. The group is swept even when the agent itself has already exited, including on Python runtimes without `waitid`, and a session whose processes all stop on SIGTERM ends at once rather than after the grace period.
