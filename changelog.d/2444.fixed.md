- **`memory_search` is about four times faster on a hub-sized store (#2444).** Each search used to re-check every note's eligibility (tenant, deletion, filters, exact tags) about twenty times, once inside each lexical, semantic and graph statement, and the tag check is a Python callback. It now checks once per search and every leg reads that result. Its two bookkeeping writes now share one commit. On a synthetic 3,000-note store with chunked embeddings, warm p50 fell from 1,951 ms to under 500 ms. Results, order, scores and diagnostics are unchanged. `scripts/bench_memory_search.py` reports the time spent in each leg.
