- **Design panels and a friction rollup (#2399).** `coordination_manage` gains
  `panel_open`, `panel_answer`, `panel_get`, `panel_list`, `panel_reveal`,
  `panel_decide` and `panel_cancel`: a design question (never code review)
  goes to 2-7 seats with the same neutral dispatch, answers stay sealed until
  all are revealed together, and the outcome is a recorded decision with its
  reasons, not a tally. It also gains `friction_report`, `friction_rollup`,
  `friction_claim_fixed`, `friction_accept` and `friction_recheck`: friction is
  ranked by severity, count and channel diversity, and a fix claim closes only
  when a re-check finds no new report, or when a human accepts it. Queue
  hygiene reveals panels past their deadline, ingests session-end friction
  nudges and runs the re-check. Ingest reads unread sessions oldest first and
  caps the reports it writes, so a burst drains over later passes, and a due
  claim is not closed while any session is still unread.
