- **Agent sessions are tenant-scoped (#1995).** Sessions now record the
  organization that opened them, and sessions opened before this change take
  their task's organization. Listing, reading, heartbeating, recalling and
  ending a session only reach your own organization's sessions. Another
  organization's session behaves exactly like a missing one, so
  `session_list` no longer shows its agents or worktree paths, and
  `session_end` can no longer write into it. The untenanted private hub
  behaves as before. A tenanted caller also
  cannot open a session on another organization's task, and a resume packet's
  experience records list only its own organization's sessions. Sessions with
  no task that were opened before this change have no organization, so a
  tenanted caller no longer sees them.
