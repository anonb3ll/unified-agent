- **Offsite backups to S3-compatible storage, scheduled on a hosted hub (#2585).**
  `UA_BACKUP_S3_URI` copies each verified snapshot to Cloudflare R2 or any
  S3-compatible bucket with one SigV4-signed HTTPS PUT, with no CLI needed. On a
  hosted container, `UA_BACKUP_INTERVAL_S` starts `unified-agent-backup-loop`
  beside the hub, and `UA_BACKUP_KEEP_DAILY` / `UA_BACKUP_KEEP_WEEKLY` bound the
  local copies on a small volume.
