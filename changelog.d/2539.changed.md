- **The add-agent page waits for the first connection with one held read
  (#2539).** Its live check now asks the hub's `GET /api/agents/{label}?wait_for=connected`
  long-poll (up to 25 s, counting only a connection at or after the token's
  `issued_at`) and asks again until it is satisfied, instead of reading the
  agent every 5 s. A busy hub (429) or a failed read is retried with a jittered
  backoff, and leaving the page or changing the signed-in owner aborts the read.
