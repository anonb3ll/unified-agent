- **Listing `coordination_manage` or `relay_manage` in `UA_APPROVAL_TOOLS`
  costs one approval for a team boot or a relay send (#1896).** Their bodies
  force their own gate (`team_up`, `relay.public.send`), and the generic gate
  used to spend a pass first, so one action needed three approvals. The
  generic gate now skips exactly those two actions; every other action of
  both tools is still gated when listed.
