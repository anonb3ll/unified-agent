- **Claude dispatch settings files no longer pile up in `dispatch-runs` (#2191).**
  A run's per-run settings file and identity sidecar are now removed when it
  ends: by the detached supervisor once the child exits, by the spawner when
  the launch is refused (host sandbox probe, missing binary, failed
  supervisor), and by the executor backend after its run. Queue hygiene also
  expires any left behind after `UA_DISPATCH_SETTINGS_RETENTION_DAYS` (default
  7; `0` keeps them), skipping runs that are still live. Launch logs are
  evidence and are never deleted by either path. Files are removed by identity, not
  name, so a resumed run that reuses the run_id keeps its own files. Only
  entries directly inside a real `dispatch-runs` directory are removed, and
  symlinks are never followed.
