- **Routine skip ledger (#2396).** Every skipped or suppressed `routine_runs`
  row now carries a typed `reason` (`overlap`, `no_signal`, `self_echo` or
  `catch_up_none`) and a `subject_key` naming what the decision was about. A
  slot passed over by `catch_up=none` now leaves a receipt. `routine_tick`
  reports `skip_reasons`, `routine_list(recent_runs=N)` reads the receipts, and
  older rows are backfilled once (`no_activity` becomes `no_signal`). The
  routine timer is not part of this change.
