- **Control Center sign-in with WorkOS, and sealed-session refresh (#2114).**
  The sign-in screen offers "Sign in with WorkOS" when the hub's WorkOS leg is
  configured (`GET /api/session/options`). `/api/session/workos/login` sends the
  browser to AuthKit for the one organization with a signed, browser-bound
  state and PKCE; the callback verifies membership and the identity map and
  mints the normal `ua_ui` session. The WorkOS session stays on the hub, so
  every write re-checks membership live and approvals are stamped
  `workos-idp-v1`. Held WorkOS sessions (that one and the OAuth approver
  cookie) are now refreshed through the SDK instead of failing about five
  minutes after sign-in. A hosted hub no longer runs `tailscale whois` on
  Railway's CGNAT edge-proxy peers (`UA_TAILNET_PEER_MODE`, forced `off` when
  hosted). `system_doctor` recognises a WorkOS-configured hub and reports the
  leg as "configured (not verified live)". Runbook: `docs/deploy/railway.md`.

- Recovery #2116 also refuses legacy passphrase approval on hosted public hubs,
  verifies callbacks cannot select another organization, and records the three
  new public sign-in routes in the route inventory. The Railway runbook now
  requires an explicit human operator label/name before first boot.

- A failed database revocation keeps the WorkOS authentication marker, so a
  copied browser cookie cannot lose its live membership checks during logout
  or an identity-map change.

- Successful sign-out retains a WorkOS authentication-kind tombstone after
  erasing its credential. In-flight requests cannot downgrade to a plain UI
  session, and an overlapping refresh cannot revive the signed-out credential.
