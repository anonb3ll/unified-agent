- **A no_effect re-check is fenced (#1986).** Reconciling an effect already recorded as `no_effect` now raises `StaleInvocation` unless `expected_fence` matches the fence the record was written under, as every other reconcile does. A stale caller no longer reaches the provider lookup or gets the record back. The `_record_no_effect` docstring now says that `no_effect` ends sending but a later fenced lookup can still upgrade it to `completed`.
