- **An approval decision whose receipt intent failed to write now still gets its receipt (#2484).** `approval_resolve` writes the requester's receipt intent behind a savepoint, so a failed insert let the decision commit with no intent row, and the receipt sweep read only the intent table: that decision never produced a receipt, however often the sweep ran. The sweep now reconciles first. For each decision of the tenant since a stored watermark that the approval row still implies and that has its resolution event but neither an intent nor a receipt, it re-creates the intent and delivers it in the same pass. The receipt keeps its permanent key `approval-receipt:<id>:<decision>` and the message its fixed id, so a replay yields one receipt and one message, never two. The sweep result reports `reconciled` when it repaired any. On the first pass after this deploys, decisions from the previous 7 days that never had a receipt get one. The watermark moves only past decisions that have their intent or receipt, so one that cannot be repaired yet stays in reach however long that takes (#2494); the first pass on a store starts one day back.
