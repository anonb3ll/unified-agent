- **A handoff can carry typed fields, and the receiver can ground them (#2390).**
  `message_send(act="handoff", handoff={...})` takes `done`, `next_action`,
  `why`, `open_questions`, `evidence` and `reviewed_head_sha` beside the body,
  and `message_list` rows return them. `coordination_manage(action="handoff_ground")`
  checks each claim against live state: T0 a human message or git, T1 CI or PR
  state, T2 anything agent-written, with a verdict of verified, mismatch or
  insufficient in at most 15 resolver calls. A high-risk `next_action` (merge,
  deploy, release, ...) needs one verified T0 or T1 claim. Report mode: an
  untyped handoff is still sent, with a warning, and grounding never blocks;
  each verdict is recorded on `agent.message.grounding` to measure first.
