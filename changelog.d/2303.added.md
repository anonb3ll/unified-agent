- **Slack approval cards (#2303).** With `UA_APPROVAL_SLACK_CHANNEL` set to an
  allowlisted channel, each new pending approval posts a Block Kit card (id,
  tool, requester, run, created, pass lifetime, `args_sha256`) with one button
  that opens it in the Control Center, and a decision posts a follow-up
  threaded under it. Cards carry no approve or deny action and never the raw
  payload. Deliveries are durable intents that survive a restart and honour
  `Retry-After`; every attempt and every loss is an `approval.delivery`
  evidence event, and `system_doctor` adds the `approval_slack_cards` row.
