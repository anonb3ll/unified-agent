- **JWK member trivia (#2165).** A block comment between a member name and its colon is legal JavaScript and is now read, so `d/*gap*/:"<key>"` is redacted, audited and blocked on all four consumers. A body holding a quote, brace or colon is left unconsumed, so a literal hidden in comment prose stays visible, and repeated trivia after a member name stays linear. A `//` line comment is deliberately not a gap: its prose has no closing delimiter, so a prose colon could be read as the member's own separator and hide the JWK beside it (tracked in #2366).
