- **`message_send` refuses a forged conversation id (#1982).** An explicit `conversation_id` must now be 1-200 letters, digits, `.`, `_`, `:` or `-`; anything else, such as `]` or a newline that could forge a `[plan: ...]` marker where the id is shown, is refused before anything is written. Generated ids are unchanged, and the Control Center composer still does not accept a `conversation_id` field.
