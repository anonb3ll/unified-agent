- **`scripts/bench_memory_search.py` reports the workload it measured
  (#2488).** A rerun with another `--notes` or `--seed` in the same `--work`
  found the earlier run's notes, saved nothing, and reported them under the
  new label. The store now records its workload before the first note is
  saved, and a run that asks for a different one exits with the reason. A
  store from before the record is reused only when its one completion marker
  and its note count both match. `peak_rss_mb` was 1024 times too high on
  macOS, where `ru_maxrss` is in bytes; it is now normalized by platform.
