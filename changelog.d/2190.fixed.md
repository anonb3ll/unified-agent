- **The trusted-proxy socket is never taken over from a live hub (#2190).** The
  hub holds an exclusive advisory lock on `<UA_HTTP_PROXY_SOCKET>.lock` for its
  whole life. A second hub that finds the lock held refuses to start, even when
  the first hub is between bind and listen, or (macOS) has a full accept queue,
  since both cases answer a probe connect the way a stale socket does. Without
  the lock, a probe connect still refuses a live listener or an inconclusive
  probe. The socket now listens before the ASGI lifespan runs. A crashed hub
  releases its lock on exit, so restarts replace the stale socket as before.
