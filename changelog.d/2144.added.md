- **Per-tool call allowance (#2144).** A read-only view of the policy gate's
  per-caller rate limits: `observability_manage("tool_allowance")`,
  `GET /api/tool-allowance`, and a *Tool allowance* section in the Control
  Center Quota pane each report, per tool, the calls charged in the live
  window, the configured limit, remaining, and — once stopped — when the
  window frees a slot, plus today's allowed-call count from the decision
  ledger. Numbers come from the same in-process counter enforcement uses
  (`gateway.rate_limit_snapshot`), not a second table. Buckets are per
  concrete tool, so glob rules keep a full-remaining pattern row for
  uncharged matching tools; superseded rule generations never report
  `stopped`, and a single subject's `stopped` also requires the gate's
  bind/roles filter to still apply to its current scope. The view is
  tenant-fenced to the calling identity; the verified operator may read any
  identity or the whole fleet.
