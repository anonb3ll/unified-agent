- **`record_recall_hits` no longer commits a connection it was handed
  (#2487).** Called with an idle connection, its savepoint was the outermost
  one, so releasing it committed the recall votes and the quality-log row, and
  a later rollback by the caller could not undo them. The helper now opens a
  transaction first on an idle connection and leaves the commit to the caller.
  `memory_search`, the only caller that passes a connection, already commits
  after the call, so its writes are stored as before.
