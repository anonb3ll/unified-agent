- **Fleet contract (`ethos.md`, #2319).** Adds a durable, reviewable statement of
  what the developer-agent fleet working in `~/dev` through the hub is meant to
  do: its role, the two outcomes it is accountable for, scope and non-goals, the
  judgment calls that apply when no written rule covers a case, ranked success
  criteria, trade-off order, hard constraints, metric semantics and vision.
  Records the standing spend policy and its claim-path enforcement rather than a
  dollar figure, notes where a claimed measure is not yet computed, and carries
  its own open questions. Documentation only; no runtime behavior changes.
