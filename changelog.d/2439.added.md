- **Component map and checker (#2437, #2439).** Every module in
  `src/unified_agent` is now placed in one of 16 components
  (`docs/architecture/components.toml`), and `scripts/check_components.py`
  fails on an import that crosses components without a declared dependency.
  Today's 75 such imports, 33 of them out of the kernel, are recorded in a
  baseline that may only shrink. `--report` prints modules, lines and edge
  counts per component. The check runs in `scripts/local-gate.sh`; see
  `docs/architecture/components.md`.
