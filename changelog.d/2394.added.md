- **A requesting agent gets a durable receipt when its approval is decided (#2394).** Deciding an approval writes a delivery intent in the same transaction. A savepoint isolates it, so a receipt failure never blocks a decision. After the commit, one `agent.approval.receipt` event is appended under the permanent key `approval-receipt:<id>:<decision>` (ids and the decision only), re-read from the approvals table rather than trusted from the intent. Only then is the intent deleted. Queue hygiene sweeps whatever a crash left behind.
