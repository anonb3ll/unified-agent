- **Routine activity gate (#1752).** `routine_add` takes `activity_gate`
  (`always` by default, or `require_activity`) and `activity_gate_scope`
  (`org` or `project`). A gated routine whose scope saw no task or event
  activity since its previous fire records a `suppressed` receipt with
  `reason=no_activity` and creates no task; its schedule still advances. The
  tasks any routine created, their events and routine bookkeeping events never
  count as activity, so two gated routines cannot keep each other firing.
