- **Tasks carry a stage, a resume capsule and graded checks (#2398).**
  `coordination_manage` gains `task_workflow_get`, `task_workflow_set`,
  `task_check_record` and `task_check_verify`. A check is `attested` when an
  agent records it, `verified` only when the hub resolves its CI run on the same
  head with the stated conclusion, and `unknown` at a head nobody recorded it
  for. A reference may name the CI `job` the check stands for: it is then
  graded on that job, and a missing or skipped job contradicts the claim.
  Without one the grade covers the whole run only and is marked
  `verified_scope: "run"`. `session_packet` returns the record as `workflow`. When a PR head moves
  off every recorded review read, the `unified-agent/review-head` check now
  names why (`rebase-identical`, `rebase-changed`, `new-commits`, `unknown`) and
  who owns the next gate, and records it on `agent.review.head_change`; its
  conclusion is unchanged (report mode).
