- The review-head gate and its check now follow a recorded read's verdict, not only its existence (#2420). A read recorded as `fix-first`, `hold`, `fail`, `pass?` or any wording that names no pass now fails the head it names, any delta read or clean push counted from that head, and every pass recorded before it; only a read recorded afterwards clears it. Negative reads stay recorded as written, the preflight refuses with the verdict named, and the merge receipt reports the rejection as a finding instead of full coverage.
