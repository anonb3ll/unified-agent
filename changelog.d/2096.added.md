- **Memory graph leg (#2096).** Notes now carry persisted entities
  (`memory_entities`: the task, branch, project, service and agent identifiers
  they name) and typed links (`memory_links`: `supersedes`, `contradicts`,
  `extends`, `derives`, `evidence`), written in the save's own transaction.
  Supersedes, contradicts and `memory:<id>` evidence refs become links on
  their own; `memory_save(links=["extends:<id>", "derives:<id>"])` adds
  explicit ones. A batched, idempotent post-init backfill fills both tables
  for existing notes. `memory_search` gains a `graph` leg: one hop from the
  top lexical/semantic hits over links and shared identifiers, under fan-out
  and hub caps, honouring supersede and `as_of` validity, fused by RRF with
  `UA_MEMORY_WEIGHT_GRAPH` (0.5). It only adds notes the content legs missed,
  so the #1913 eval is unchanged. `memory_get` returns `links: {out, in}`.
  Every write and read is fenced to the caller's org; DB triggers refuse a
  cross-org row.
  Historical edge filtering now follows the raw scan cap, so queries before
  most edges were recorded retain bounded work. Legacy backfill derives text
  outside the write lock, verifies changed snapshots, and commits small
  batches with their cursor without blocking writers during extraction.
  Legacy BLOB text no longer aborts graph backfill at startup, and persisted
  relation arrays are capped before taking the write lock.
  Backfill skips legacy non-text IDs without rewriting identity or preventing
  subsequent notes from being processed.
