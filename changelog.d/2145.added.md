- **LongMemEval retrieval scorecard (#2145).** `scripts/eval_longmemeval.py`
  runs the public LongMemEval-S benchmark against production `memory_search`
  and `vault_query`. Each question gets a fresh temporary store, every haystack
  session is saved through `memory_save`, and the harness scores whether the
  evidence sessions come back in the top 5 and top 10. The dataset is pinned by
  revision and sha256. `docs/evals/longmemeval.md` publishes the settings,
  per-category scores, sample size and seed, misses, wall time and rerun
  commands, with a comparison against GBrain's published LongMemEval figure.
