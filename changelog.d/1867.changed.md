- **Approval Ledger rows follow a push within minutes, and say who made them.**
  The `pr-watch` job now decides each launch what is due: the journal and the
  Approval Ledger poll every 30 minutes, and the ledger poll every
  `UA_APPROVAL_LEDGER_ACTIVE_INTERVAL_S` (default 120 s) while a ledger row is
  pending, or approved inside its TTL, for a PR head the last poll saw open.
  Overlapping launches take a lock and run one tick. A launch with nothing
  due makes no GitHub call, so the
  LaunchAgent can be launched every 2 minutes; at the current 30-minute
  interval nothing changes until that is done. `unified-agent-review ledger
  poll` (and `POST /api/approvals/ledger-poll`, the `approval_ledger_poll`
  coordination action) runs one poll on the hub now, for operators only; it
  requests rows and never resolves one. Worker-created rows are attributed to
  the `approval_ledger_worker` service identity instead of `agent-codex`
  once its bearer is registered (task #1867).
