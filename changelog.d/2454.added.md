- **Ready dependents are told (#2454).** When a task moves to `done` or
  `cancelled`, each dependent it makes ready gets one `inform` from `hub-graph`
  addressed to its next owner, claimant or lane agent, once per readiness
  generation, with cancelled prerequisites named. Unowned ready work is counted
  as `queue_health.ready_unowned`. `UA_READY_DEPENDENT_NOTIFY=0` turns it off.
