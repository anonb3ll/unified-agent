- **Evidence gaps fail loud (#1672).** Approving one of the six separation-of-duties tools (`work_product_release`, `acp_permission`, `harness_config_apply`, `config_revision_rollback`, `config_revision_commit`, `org_proposal_apply`) now requires the resolver's signed producer stamp. The stamp is written in the same transaction as the approval. Without it the request stays pending and the error names the gap. A verified human without a `UA_AGENT_TOKENS` bearer or WorkOS mapping can no longer approve those tools. Other resolutions still complete, and a missing stamp records `audit.evidence.gap`. The gap appears on `/api/attention` as `evidence_gap`, in the digest's `waiting_on_operator`, and on the Watch as a generic anomaly.
