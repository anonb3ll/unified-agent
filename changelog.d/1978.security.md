- **Mission autonomy settings are fenced per tenant (#1978).** On an enterprise
  hub, the autonomy tables a mission uses (mode, standing grants, revisions,
  pending actions) now record their tenant. Another tenant's mission reads as
  an unknown mission: it can no longer be read, have its mode or grants
  changed, be evaluated against, or have its pending actions reviewed or
  started. The private untenanted hub behaves as before.
