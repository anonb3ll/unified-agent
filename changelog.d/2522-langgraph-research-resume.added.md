- **Resume a paused LangGraph research run (task #2522, #2498 item q).** `langgraph_run(run_id=..., resume=True)` continues a research run paused for its approval, and `POST /api/action/langgraph/run` with `engine: "research"` does the same from the browser. `PilotRunner.load_run` rebuilds the run from its latest checkpoint with its original claim, task revision, profile, budget and trace, and refuses a missing, corrupt or other-revision checkpoint. Only the run's owner or the verified operator may resume it, and only for a task in the caller's tenant; the run always executes under its owner's saved profile and current ceiling, and every authority check, the policy gate and the approval gate run again; the budget revision is pinned to the hub's autonomy limits and is never refreshed. A heartbeat-renewed lease in the checkpoint store (`langgraph_resume_leases`) admits one resume at a time, and every checkpoint write is fenced on its token, so a holder that lost its lease records nothing. Results are typed (`outcome`: `run_completed` only with a real output, `approval_interrupted`, `run_denied`, `run_failed`, `run_conflict`, each refusal with a `code`).
