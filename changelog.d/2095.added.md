- **Memory notes have bi-temporal validity (#2095).** A supersede, through
  `memory_save(supersedes=...)` or the consolidate apply step, now stamps the
  old note's new `valid_to` column (the replacement's `valid_from`, else the
  supersede time) instead of only hiding it; restore clears it. Nothing is
  deleted. `memory_search` and `memory_get` take `as_of`, an ISO datetime with
  a UTC offset (naive times are refused), and answer for that instant,
  including notes superseded later. The recency leg dates a note by its
  `valid_from` when set. Without `as_of` results are unchanged; the #1913
  ranking eval is identical before and after.
