- **Weekly export no longer flagged as a silent agent (#1969).** The anomaly
  check held every agent label to one 2-day journal window, so the weekly
  export job was reported silent five days of every week.
  `UA_ANOMALY_SILENT_AGENT_DAYS_BY_LABEL` (default `weekly-export-cron=8`) now
  gives a label its own window, and a missed weekly run still alerts after 8
  days. A silent-agent threshold that cannot form a date, such as `inf` or a
  huge number, now falls back to the default instead of stopping the whole
  anomaly check.
