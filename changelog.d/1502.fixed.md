- **Sleep pass keeps its reviews when a model call fails (#1502).** A single reply
  that missed the verdict schema, or one transient provider error, used to abort
  the nightly pass after dozens of good reviews (exit 1, no report). Each cluster
  now gets one retry. A cluster that still fails is left for a later run, and the
  pass writes its report and finishes `partial` (exit 2, `error_code:
  llm_review_incomplete`). Three failed clusters in a row stop the review. A
  failed journal digest after successful reviews also finishes `partial`
  (`llm_digest_failed`). If nothing could be reviewed, the pass still exits 1 and
  records `state=llm_unavailable`, which doctor's `sleep_pass_timer` row reports
  as DEGRADED ("ran, but no LLM route answered") instead of MISCONFIGURED
  "installed but inactive".
