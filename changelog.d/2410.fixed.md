- **A review read recorded after a merge now clears `unified-agent/review-head` (#2410).**
  The check was published only by the Approval Ledger poll, which covers open
  pull requests, so a steward who read a batch after merging it left a
  permanent false ERROR on every merged head. Recording a read now publishes
  that head itself, once, after the row commits; a failed publish is reported
  as `review_head: {status: error}` in the response and never discards the read.
  The publish answers for every open pull request on that head, stays serialized
  with the poll, and defers to it when the poll holds the lock; the poll retries
  closed heads read in the last 24 hours until one shows `success`.
