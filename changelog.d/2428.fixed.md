- **A granted idle session stays wakeable (#2428).** The hub closed a session 30 minutes after it went quiet, and wakes reach only open sessions, so a session could be woken only for its first 30 minutes of idling. A node running `UA_NODE_WAKE=1` now reports which sessions still have a pane on its wake poll, and the hub keeps requested and granted sessions on that node's own host open. One the reaper already closed reopens; a session that ended any other way stays closed.
