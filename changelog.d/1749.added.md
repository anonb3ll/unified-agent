- **Task liveness invariant (#1749).** A pure classifier names each task's
  action path (run, queued, review, approval, prerequisites, declared wait,
  monitor, human owner, or none). A sweep in the queue-hygiene pass records a
  daily digest entry for every `doing` task, and every `open` task in the lane
  of an agent not seen on the hub for 24 hours, that has none.
  `UA_LIVENESS_SWEEP=enforce` also moves such tasks to `blocked` with a
  recovery note; the default `report` moves nothing, and should run (and its
  findings be reviewed) before `enforce` is turned on. `task_update` gains
  one-shot monitors (`monitor_at`, `monitor_note`, `clear_monitor`) and
  declared waits (`declared_wait`), both at most 14 days ahead, with a UTC
  offset, and settable only by the claimant, the unclaimed task's lane agent,
  a lifecycle operator or the human operator. A chain of waits longer than 28
  days is reported to the digest. Self-pickup tasks whose last three runs in
  30 days lapsed their lease are escalated like dead-lettered delegations.
