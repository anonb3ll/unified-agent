- **Agents can propose a batch of work; the operator approves it item by item (#2393).** `coordination_manage(action="work_proposal_file")` files up to 50 items, each with a title, notes and a repo/file scope. Only the human operator can decide them, through `POST /api/action/work-proposal-decide` in the Control Center or a human MCP identity, and may edit an item while approving it. Approval freezes a contract digest and spawns one task. `task_claim` refuses a proposal task whose title, notes or scope drifted. Items stuck mid-approval recover forward or roll back on the queue-hygiene pass. Reporting defaults to final-only.
