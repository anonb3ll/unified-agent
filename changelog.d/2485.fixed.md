- **`handoff_ground` no longer reports a real reference as proof of a claimed approval (#2485).** A handoff that cited an unrelated but existing commit with the claim "Operator approved deployment" came back `verdict: verified` and `high_risk_met: true`: the resolver had checked that the commit exists, and the report presented that as the sender's claim being true. Each claim now reports `checked` (the predicate the resolver evaluated) and `reference` (its result) apart from the sender's `claim`, and the report has an `authorization` field: `not_claimed`, or `not_checked` whenever the handoff says an approval was given. `authorization_checked` is always `false`, because no cited message is read; `authorization_reference` is `operator_message` when the operator's own message is cited, as the place for a person to read it (#2495). While it is `not_checked`, a claim worded as that approval is `insufficient`, the verdict is not `verified` and `high_risk_met` is false. The same holds when the approval is asserted in `done` or `why` instead of an evidence item. Handoffs that assert no approval ground exactly as before. Report mode still: nothing is granted, refused or gated.
