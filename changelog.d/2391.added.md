- **Messages report what actually happened to them (#2391).** `message_list` rows carry `delivery`: admitted (stored), appended (a wake rang a live session of the recipient, from the wake receipts; `unknown` when the node could not confirm it), read, and terminal (answered, acked or dead letter). The old `status` stays for existing readers. Attention mail (a request, query or handoff, or `expects_reply`) still unread 24 hours after it was stored becomes a dead letter once, and its sender gets one notice from `hub-delivery` naming it by id; nothing is re-sent. `UA_MESSAGE_DEAD_LETTER` (off, report or notify, default notify), `UA_MESSAGE_DEAD_LETTER_HOURS` and `UA_MESSAGE_DEAD_LETTER_WINDOW_HOURS` (default 48, so no historical backlog is swept) control it.
