- **Time-dependent tests no longer fail on a slow or late CI shard (#2201).**
  The task-liveness idempotence test swept at "now + 2h" and again five
  minutes later; when CI ran just before 22:00 UTC the second sweep fell on a
  new UTC day and the task was correctly reported again. Its helper now keeps
  the sweep time clear of midnight. Six redaction linearity tests that
  asserted a fixed wall-clock bound (1.5-5 s) now compare CPU-time growth on
  a 4x input with `growth_budget.assert_cpu_growth`, the check #2188 adopted,
  so a loaded shard cannot fail them while a quadratic scan still does.
