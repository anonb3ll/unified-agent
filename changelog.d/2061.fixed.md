- **A task reopened in the same second as its close keeps a newer revision (#2061).**
  Every writer of `tasks.updated_at` (merge reopen and retain, the reviewer's
  notes and scout flip, routine release, queue hygiene, doctor and agent-lifecycle
  claim release, executor lease renewal, plan and red-team notes) now stamps a
  strictly increasing microsecond revision, read and written under one writer
  lock, so two concurrent writers can never stamp the same revision and
  `task_update(expected_updated_at=...)` never passes after a change the caller
  did not see. A source tripwire test fails CI if a new writer stamps
  second-precision time or computes a revision without that fence.
