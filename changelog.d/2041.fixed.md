- **Claim-time worktrees come from the task's project, never the cockpit (#2041).** `task_claim` and `task_update(status="doing")` used to cut an unmarked `unified-agent` task's worktree from `UA_WORKSPACE` (the cockpit) and store a relative `.worktrees/task-<id>` path, so product branches landed in the cockpit repo. Only `[code-task]` rows reached the product checkout. The project's configured checkout now decides the repo whatever the notes say, and `worktree_path` is always stored absolute. When no product checkout resolves on the host, provisioning is skipped and `task_claim` returns `provisioning: {"status": "skipped", "reason": "no_product_repo"}`. Legacy relative rows still release from the workspace.
