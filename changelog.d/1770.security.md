- **A team boot binds each seat's checkout real path, not only its path string (#1770).** The plan, the `team_up` approval and the queued node item carry `cwd_realpath`, and ua-node refuses to launch (`cwd_realpath_changed`) when a symlinked checkout was retargeted after approval, even to a directory still inside its worktree roots. Restarting or resuming such an item, or restoring its seat from a team snapshot, keeps the binding, and the plan text shows the bound real path. A bound checkout runs only on a node that advertises `cwd_realpath`: planning picks such a node when the label has one and otherwise refuses with `cwd_realpath_unsupported`, and approval, enqueue and claim refuse an older node.
