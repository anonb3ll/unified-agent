- **New Control Center look (#1942).** The Control Center uses the Catppuccin
  Mocha palette in dark mode and a contrast-corrected Latte palette in light
  mode, with self-hosted Geist Sans and Geist Mono. Buttons use mauve as the
  single action colour; rose now means only an error or a block. Waiting
  sessions show amber instead of looking like running work, and every text
  colour pair is checked to at least 4.5:1 in all three themes.
