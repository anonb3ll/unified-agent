- **Tenancy slice 2: org_id indexes and the #1433 key constraints on every hub.**
  Eighteen partitioned tables gain an index leading on `org_id`. Existing
  hubs are rebuilt once, at the next start, so `task_comments.task_id`
  references `tasks(id) ON DELETE CASCADE` and seven text primary keys are
  `NOT NULL`, the shape fresh hubs now get from `CREATE TABLE`. The rebuild
  deletes orphan comments and NULL-key rows, writes a verified copy of the
  affected tables to `UA_BACKUP_DIR/constraints-2326-<stamp>.db` first,
  commits with one `agent.storage.constraints_rebuilt` evidence event, and
  rolls back fully on any error. `unified-agent db migrate-constraints
  --dry-run` reports the rows it would delete without changing anything.
  No `SCHEMA_VERSION` bump.
