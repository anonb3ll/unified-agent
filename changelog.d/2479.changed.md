- **`/ui/` now opens the new Control Center (#2479).** The eight destinations
  (Home, Inbox, Work, Agents, Sessions, Knowledge, Runs, Settings) replace the
  39 classic views, and every page has its own address. Old links keep
  working: `/ui/?view=<id>` and `/ui/#<id>`, including the older aliases,
  open the matching new page, and an approval, task or plan the link named
  opens directly. Approval push notifications, Slack approval buttons, URL
  elicitation and task-completion messages now link to the new pages.
