- **Default test runs sample the redaction and JWK matrices more thinly (#2261).**
  A plain `pytest` run, locally and in CI, now keeps a representative subset of
  each large redaction, JWK, secret and audit parametrize matrix: every value of
  every dimension at least once, plus a deterministic slice of about one case in
  100. The default collection drops from about 27,700 to about 23,400 of the
  suite's 71,800 cases. `UA_TEST_EXHAUSTIVE=1` or `--exhaustive` still runs
  every case, and CI still forces it for any change that can alter those
  matrices; `--param-sample=pairwise` keeps the previous pairwise tier.
