- **Worktree idleness follows git activity (#2177).** `cleanup_stale_worktrees`
  and the worktree survey judge `idle_timeout` by the newest mtime of the
  checkout root and its gitdir's `index`, `HEAD`, `logs/HEAD`, `FETCH_HEAD`,
  `ORIG_HEAD` and `MERGE_HEAD`. A worktree where someone is staging,
  committing, checking out, fetching or merging is no longer called idle just
  because its root directory did not change. The survey reads those times
  before running git, and its `git status` uses `--no-optional-locks`, so a
  survey never makes a worktree look active by refreshing its index. A
  worktree removed mid-scan no longer aborts the survey.
