- **The classic Control Center is gone (#2479).** Its panes, shell and the
  "Back to classic" links are deleted; `docs/web-ui.md#classic-links` maps
  each view to its new page. Every action the classic views offered has a
  place in the new app: sprints and per-run operating settings on
  Agents › Teams › Operating, restarting the executor, revoking a device's
  sign-in, approver passkeys and attesting a device's acceptance on
  Agents › Hosts, policy revisions and rollback on Agents › Org chart, and
  skill install, publish, team install and starter templates on Agents ›
  Skills and Teams.
