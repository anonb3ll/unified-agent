- **Approval Ledger statuses no longer wait for the next poll after a decision (#2234).**
  Approving or denying a `github_app.process_pr_event` row on the HTTP hub now
  runs the poll's own per-PR step for that pull request at once, on a background
  worker under the worker's service identity: the live head gets its success
  status and signed evidence comment (or its failure) within seconds instead of
  at the next `pr-watch` poll. A superseded head is retired as stale, never
  approved: the poll and this path re-read the PR right before and right after
  a success and turn it `stale` if the head moved. A success stays visible only
  once its evidence is written; any failure after the POST withdraws it to
  `action_required`. Every path settles an approved head through one decision
  over the approved and live head and base, and the scheduled poll reconciles
  consumed approvals with no final record by PR number, least recently
  attempted first (success and evidence at the approved head and base, open
  or closed; `stale` otherwise). A closed PR is left to the
  poll, which stays the backstop and repeats nothing. The `pr-watch` tick
  planner also sends one conditional request (`If-None-Match`, free when GitHub
  answers 304) on a launch past the active interval with no row in flight, and
  polls only when an open PR head moved (a full first page or a next-page link
  always polls), so the job can be launched every two minutes cheaply. That
  needs the LaunchAgent's `StartInterval` lowered from 1800 to 120; until then
  a new head still waits for the 30-minute poll.
