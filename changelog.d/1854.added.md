- Operators can re-request the Approval Ledger row for an open PR head that a poll missed: `coordination_manage(approval_ledger_rerequest)` or `POST /api/approvals/ledger-rerequest` with the PR number and its current head (the route runs the same action, so its profile ceiling, policy rules and approval gate apply; the verified operator passes the profile ceiling only). The request is checked against the live head, goes through the connector policy like a scheduled poll, queues the approval without resolving or spending it, and is recorded on `agent.github_app.rerequested`. A row whose pending status failed to post is found by its PR head, even after a title or label edit, and gets its status bound instead of a second row, by the re-request or by the next poll; unbound rows for older heads are retired when the PR moves on; and an older live decision for a head whose newest row was revoked or expired is refused, by the re-request and the poll, rather than bound or spent (#1854).
