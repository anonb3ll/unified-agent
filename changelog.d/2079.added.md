- **Commit identities are checkable before they land (#2079).**
  `scripts/check_commit_identity.py` fails when a commit in a range (default
  `origin/main..HEAD`) has an author or committer that is not a fleet label at
  an allowed address or an accepted `Name <address>` pair, or that looks like
  git's auto-detected `user@host.lan` fallback. Each failure names the commit
  and the fix without printing the rejected name or address.
