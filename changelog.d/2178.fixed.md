- **In-use worktree detection follow-ups (#2178).** Liveness now resolves
  relative command-line paths against the process's cwd and a bare argv[0]
  against its `PATH` (on Linux from `/proc`, elsewhere from `lsof` cwd plus
  `ps`). A refused `/proc` read on a process with the hub's own credentials
  makes liveness unknown instead of being skipped, and the reason names the
  first three such processes (`pid <n> (<comm>)`). An unlistable `/proc` is
  unknown rather than an error. A done, blocked or orphaned task's worktree
  that a process still uses is reported stale with `in_use: true` and
  retained, never removed. When processes cannot be listed, cleanup reports
  `liveness_unknown` with a note naming the cause, and the `system_doctor`
  `stale_worktrees` row reads DEGRADED with that cause instead of silently
  removing nothing. The deploy-checkout guard deliberately stays a bare
  `release-`/`rollback-` prefix match: deploy names have no fixed grammar,
  and over-matching only keeps a worktree.
