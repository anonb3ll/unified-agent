- **Control Center Refresh never shares an earlier doctor call (#1897).** A
  forced `/api/health?force=1` request no longer joins a hub call that began
  before it arrived, forced or not, whose worktree survey and other probes
  could predate the change the operator just made, nor a reading published
  while it took the state lock. Forced requests that arrive before the queued
  call starts share it (a burst costs at most two calls; a continuing stream
  costs one per start). Every doctor call runs as its own task, so a closed
  request does not fail the others; the slot passes to the queued call
  atomically when the call ahead finishes; and every wait is bounded: after
  300 s a request gets the last reading with an error, or a `TimeoutError`
  (HTTP 500) when there is no reading yet. The queued call's wait behind a
  stalled call is bounded too. Ordinary polls still share whatever call is in
  flight.
