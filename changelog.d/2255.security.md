- **Telemetry spans export only allowlisted metadata (#2255).** `TelemetrySpan`
  and the OTLP serializer keep the fixed `EXPORTED_METADATA_KEYS` as scalars.
  Tool arguments, results, paths and other free text are dropped and only
  counted (`_dropped`). A secret-bearing value becomes an opaque per-process
  `secret-ref:` rather than a guessable hash, and `error_message` is withheld.
  `UA_TELEMETRY_FREEFORM_METADATA=1` restores the earlier redacted free-form
  export. OTLP spans carry approval id, policy version and hash, device key
  fingerprint and artifact SHA as `ua.*` attributes when a producer knows them.
