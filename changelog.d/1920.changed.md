- **pr-watch no longer floods the daily journal (#1920).** Each PR event's
  journal line now goes to a per-day note, `Logs/pr-watch/<YYYY-MM-DD>.md`
  (same line format and entry cap as `vault_journal`). The shared daily
  journal gets at most one pr-watch line a day, a pointer to that note.
  Previously pr-watch wrote most of the daily journal's lines on busy days.
