- **Session-scoped hub MCP credentials are hardened before they are switched on
  (#1893, #1744).** The node now redacts a credential split across streamed
  tool-output updates. The hub redacts node event text before cutting it to
  size, scrubs every field of a permission prompt (so a re-polled prompt still
  matches its approval), and no longer reports a stale revoke reason for a
  rolled-back revocation. `memory_save` and `task_comment` redact a pasted
  `uas_` credential from free text (with a warning in the result) and refuse it
  in structured fields. A refresh that races its own expiry, or fails after the
  store rotated the token, no longer leaves an orphan sign-in row; a
  single-session revoke removes every row of that sign-in family; and one
  malformed sign-in row no longer refuses every credential of its label.
