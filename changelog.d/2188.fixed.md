- **Scanner growth tests no longer fail under CI load (#2188).** The linear-time
  checks for the redaction and JWK scanners measure CPU time instead of wall
  time, interleave the small and large runs, keep the best sample for each
  size, and measure again before failing. Peak-memory checks collect garbage
  first and re-measure before failing. A quadratic scan still fails, and a new
  self-test proves it.
