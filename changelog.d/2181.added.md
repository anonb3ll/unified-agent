- **Operators can retire a stale ua-node row (#2181).** `coordination_manage`
  action `node_retire` (node, reason) marks a renamed or decommissioned
  device's node row retired without deleting it, and records `kind="retire"`
  on the `agent.node` ledger. A retired node is left out of the doctor's fleet
  rows, the node and fleet listing, and routing; `fleet_nodes` names and counts
  retired rows instead of reporting a stale host. Its re-registration and
  heartbeats are refused (`code: "node_retired"`; the ua-node logs it once
  and re-checks every 30 minutes) until an operator runs `node_unretire`. Lifecycle
  operators (`UA_LIFECYCLE_OPERATORS`) only; refused while the node has queued
  or claimed work.
