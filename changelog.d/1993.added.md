- **Completion return for Control Center handoffs (#1993).** A conversation handed off to a new task now records where it came from. When that task reaches done, blocked or cancelled, a short status goes back into the conversation to the person who handed it off: the task id, the status, the neutralised title and the evidence link, the same as on Slack. The notice is sent from the service label named in `UA_MESSAGES_REPORT_AGENT`, which must be on the roster, and is posted at most once per transition. Linking an existing task records nothing. When the variable is unset, notices stay pending and expire after 24 hours.
